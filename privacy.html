NutIn privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who uses NutIn

NutIn is a personal, single-user tool. Its only user is the person who built it, and it only accesses that person's own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's consent, NutIn requests read-only access to their Google Health data: activity and fitness, sleep, health metrics and measurements (such as heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, oxygen saturation, skin temperature and weight), nutrition, and profile and settings. It never writes, changes or deletes any data in the Google account.

How the data is used

The data is summarised into weekly health reports for the account owner's own review. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Where the data is stored

Summaries are kept in the account owner's private code repository. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are used only to fetch the owner's own data.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
